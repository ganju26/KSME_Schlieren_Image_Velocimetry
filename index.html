Method

Recording: 105 frames at 15 fps (7 s). Each frame was registered to the middle frame (rigid shift) and matched in global brightness. The 105-frame time mean was then subtracted, so that only the moving schlieren pattern remains, and the image was binned 4×4. The velocity between frames k and k+1 (Δt = 1/15 s) was computed with Horn–Schunck optical flow (α = 30). Dark regions are masked. Arrow length: 0.8 mm/s = one grid spacing (about 0.7 mm); faster vectors are clipped to this length. Color gives the speed (0–3 mm/s).
